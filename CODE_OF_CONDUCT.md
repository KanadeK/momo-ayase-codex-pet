# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Harassment, hate speech, sexualized
discussion of minors, threats, doxxing, impersonation, and spam are not
accepted.

Critique code and artwork decisions, not people. Respect character and artwork
rights. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and participation
that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact methods on
GitHub. Reports will be handled with discretion.
